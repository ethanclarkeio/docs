sidebarTitle: "Redeem a Credit Code"

---

DGrid Credits let you access leading models on the DGrid platform. If you received a DGrid Credit Code, follow these steps to apply it to your account.

<Steps>

<Step title="Log in to DGrid">

Go to [DGrid](https://dgrid.ai/) and connect your wallet.

<Frame caption="Connect your wallet to log in to DGrid">
  <img src="/images/dgrid-credit-login.png" alt="DGrid homepage with the sign-in option" />
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Open Billing">

Click your profile icon in the top-right corner and select **Billing**.

<Frame caption="Open Billing from the profile menu">
  <img src="/images/dgrid-credit-billing.png" alt="Billing option in the DGrid profile menu" />
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Validate your Credit Code">

On the **Billing** page, scroll to the **Deposit** section and click **Redeem Code**. Enter your code, then click **Validate Code**.

<Frame caption="Enter and validate your DGrid Credit Code">
  <img src="/images/dgrid-credit-redeem-code.png" alt="Redeem Code form in the Deposit section" />
</Frame>

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the redemption">

Review the code details and click **Confirm Redeem**. Your DGrid Credits are applied to your account immediately.

<Frame caption="Review the Credit Code details and confirm the redemption">
  <img src="/images/dgrid-credit-confirm.png" alt="DGrid Credit Code details and Confirm Redeem button" />
</Frame>

</Step>

</Steps>

After the credits are available, you can start using DGrid. If you have trouble redeeming your code, contact the [DGrid team on Telegram](https://t.me/dgrid_ai).
